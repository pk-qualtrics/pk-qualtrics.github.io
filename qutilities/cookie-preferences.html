This page explains how qUtilities uses cookies and similar technologies to support website functionality, improve website performance, and enhance your online experience.

About Cookies

qUtilities uses cookies and similar technologies to improve website functionality, personalize content, and better understand how visitors interact with our website.

Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help websites remember information about your visit, such as preferences and settings.

Essential Cookies

Essential cookies are required for the website to function properly and cannot be disabled. These cookies support core functionality such as navigation, accessibility, security, and fraud prevention.

Status: Always Active

Personalization Cookies

Personalization cookies help us remember user preferences and settings, such as selected communities, language preferences, and previous interactions with the website.

These cookies can help provide content and information that is more relevant to your interests and service area.

Analytics and Performance Cookies

Analytics and performance cookies help us understand how visitors use our website. This information may be used to identify areas for improvement and help us provide a better online experience.

How We Use Cookies

  • Maintain website functionality and security
  • Remember user preferences and settings
  • Improve website performance and usability
  • Understand how visitors use our website
  • Improve content and online services

Manage Your Preferences

You can manage your cookie preferences through your browser settings. Depending on your browser, you may be able to block, delete, or restrict cookies.

Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality and features available on the website.
