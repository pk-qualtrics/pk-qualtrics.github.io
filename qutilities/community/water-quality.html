Our Commitment to Water Quality

We conduct routine testing throughout our water system to ensure water quality meets or exceeds applicable drinking water regulations. Samples are analyzed by certified laboratories and reviewed by regulatory agencies.

Water quality can vary by service area. We encourage customers to review their local Consumer Confidence Report (CCR) for information specific to their community.
