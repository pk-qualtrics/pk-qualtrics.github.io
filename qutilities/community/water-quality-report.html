Testing Results

During the year, qUtilities collected water samples throughout the community service area to monitor the presence of biological, inorganic, and synthetic substances. All samples were analyzed by certified laboratories to ensure compliance with federal and state drinking water regulations.

The results below summarize key water quality indicators and demonstrate our continued commitment to providing safe, reliable drinking water for our customers.

Understanding the Results

Water quality standards are established by regulatory agencies to protect public health. Results reported above indicate compliance with all applicable drinking water requirements during the reporting period.

Customers may request additional information regarding testing procedures, sample collection schedules, and historical reports by contacting Customer Service.
