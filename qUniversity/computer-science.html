ABOUT THE PROGRAM

What is Computer Science?

Computer Science is the study of computers, software, algorithms, data, and the technology used to solve real-world problems. It combines logical thinking, creativity, mathematics, and programming to develop solutions that make people's lives easier.

In our Computer Science program, students learn how software is designed and developed, how computers communicate, how information is stored and processed, and how emerging technologies such as artificial intelligence are changing the world.

Students also gain practical experience by working on projects, developing applications, solving programming problems, and learning how to work as part of a technology team.

Why Study Computer Science?

01 Build valuable programming and technology skills.
02 Develop problem-solving and logical thinking abilities.
03 Work on real-world technology projects.
04 Prepare for a wide range of technology careers.
